# Description for **Wallet**
<img class="about" alt="Wallet" src="/images/Wallet.jpeg" width="50" />

One of the most crucial things I carry with me every day as a college student is my wallet. It contains necessities that I require for everyday school activities and personal errands, such my driver's license, student ID, debit and credit cards, and occasionally cash. Losing my wallet would be extremely inconvenient and stressful because it would prevent me from using dining halls, transportation, identity, and financial resources. I therefore take extra care to ensure that I never lose my wallet and always carry it with me without mis placing it.
