# Description for **Glasses**
<img class="about" alt="Glasses" src="/images/glasses.jpeg" width="50" />

I rely on prescription glasses to see clearly and perform daily activities. Without them, my vision is significantly impaired, making it difficult to read, recognize faces, or navigate safely in unfamiliar or busy environments. My glasses are essential for basic tasks such as driving, studying, and using digital devices. Because of this, I cannot leave the house without them, as doing so would compromise my safety, independence, and ability to function effectively throughout the day.
